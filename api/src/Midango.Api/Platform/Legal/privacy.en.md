# Privacy Policy

**Draft — to be reviewed by a lawyer before launch.**

This policy explains what personal data MIDANGO collects when you use the MIDANGO app, the MIDANGO Business dashboard and the MIDANGO website, why we collect it, who can see it, and the choices you have. We process personal data in line with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and its implementing regulations.

## Who we are

MIDANGO is a marketplace for booking sports courts in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf. Customers use the app to find venues (padel, football and other sports) and book a court for a time. The venues are independent businesses that run the courts and manage their bookings through the MIDANGO Business dashboard.

MIDANGO is operated by [Company legal name, CR number, address]. For this policy, we are the controller of the personal data described below.

## What we collect and why

**When you create an account and sign in:**

- Your mobile phone number. We use it to identify your account and to send you a one-time sign-in code by SMS. We do not use passwords.
- The name you give us. It is shown on your bookings so the venue knows who is coming.

**When you book:**

- Your bookings: the venue, court, date, time, price and status (for example confirmed, checked in, cancelled or no-show).
- Your cancellation history, including whether a cancellation was made after the venue's free-cancellation window (a late cancellation).
- Your no-show history. We use it to apply our fair-play rule, described below.

**To run the app on your device:**

- A push-notification token for your device, so we can send booking confirmations, reminders and updates.
- Your device language and the language you choose in the app, so we can show the app and send notifications in Arabic or English.

**When a venue asks for a demo on our website:**

- The venue name, contact name, phone number, city, number of courts and any message. We use this only to contact the venue about MIDANGO.

**What we do not collect:** payment is made in cash at the venue. We do not collect card numbers, bank details or any other payment data.

## The fair-play rule

When customers book a court and do not turn up, the venue loses that slot and other players lose the chance to book it. To keep courts available for everyone:

- Before your game, we may send a "Still coming?" reminder so you can confirm or cancel in time.
- If you miss several bookings within a period (no-shows), your account may be limited to fewer upcoming bookings at a time for a while.

This is an automated rule based only on your recorded no-shows. If you believe a no-show was recorded by mistake, contact us and we will review it.

## Legal basis

Under the PDPL we rely on:

- **Performing our agreement with you:** to create your account, sign you in, take and manage your bookings and share them with the venue.
- **Our legitimate interests:** to keep the marketplace fair and reliable (the fair-play rule and cancellation records), to prevent misuse and to keep the service secure.
- **Your consent:** for push notifications, which you can turn off at any time in your device settings.
- **Legal obligations:** where a law or a competent authority requires us to keep or disclose data.

## Who sees your data

- **Venues:** when you book at a venue, that venue sees the name you gave us, a masked version of your phone number (only the last 4 digits) and the details of your booking at that venue. Venues do not see your bookings at other venues.
- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging:** we use this Google service to deliver push notifications. It processes your device token on our behalf as a service provider.
- **Hosting provider:** our servers and database are hosted by [hosting provider, location of data]. They store data on our behalf and may not use it for their own purposes.
- **SMS provider:** [SMS provider] delivers the one-time sign-in code to your phone number.
- **Authorities:** where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

## Your rights

Under the PDPL you have the right to:

- Be informed about how your data is processed (this policy).
- Access your personal data and get a copy of it.
- Ask us to correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete. You can change your name in the app at any time.
- Ask us to delete your data when it is no longer needed, subject to what the law requires us to keep.
- Withdraw your consent at any time, for example by turning off push notifications. This does not affect processing before the withdrawal.
