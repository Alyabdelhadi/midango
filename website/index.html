For players

From your sofa to the court in three steps.

Find a venue near you, see what's free and book it. No card needed: you pay at the venue.

  1. Pick your sport

    Padel, football and more. Browse venues near you with their courts, opening hours and prices.

  2. Pick a court and time

    See open slots as they are and book the one that suits you. Your booking is confirmed straight away.

  3. Show your QR at the desk

    Every booking comes with a QR code. Show it when you arrive and pay cash at the venue.

Questions, answered.

Something else? Ask us in the demo form.

Is the app free for players?

Yes. Downloading the app and booking are free. You only pay for the court.

How do I pay?

In cash at the venue. Show your booking's QR code at the desk and pay when you arrive.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, free of charge until the venue's cancellation window starts.

What does MIDANGO cost venues?

Talk to us and we'll set your venue up. Send a demo request and we'll call you.

Which sports can I book?

Padel, football and more, depending on what each venue offers.

Is it available in Arabic?

Yes. The app and the dashboard are fully bilingual, in English and Arabic.
